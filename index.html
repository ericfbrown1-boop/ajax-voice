Ajax Voice

Interruptible voice, phone straight to OpenAI. No data access — no lookups, no figures.

Checking this browser…

Safari will ask for the microphone. The token is used in this tab only, is never stored, and is cleared once the call connects.

connecting…
Audio never touches the machine that minted the token. A call in progress ends after 30 min, or 120s of silence — a realtime socket bills per minute, idle included. That is separate from the token, which only governs how long you have to start a call.
